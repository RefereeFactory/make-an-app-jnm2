How it works

  1. Type a title and your note, pick a colour, and press Save note.
  2. Your notes are stored safely in the cloud, so they're still here next time you open the app — on any device.
  3. Use the search box to instantly find a note by its words.
  4. Press Edit on a note to change it, or Delete to remove it for good.
